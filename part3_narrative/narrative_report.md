## 3.3 Chart-Choice Justification

### 1. Which month had the highest total revenue?

*Chart type: Bar chart.*

This is a univariate categorical comparison of total revenue across three months. A bar chart makes the difference between April, May, and June easy to compare within a few seconds. The y-axis should start at zero, and no 3D effect is needed.

### 2. What percentage share does Ethnic Wear represent of April's total revenue?

*Chart type: Pie chart.*

This is a part-to-whole question: Ethnic Wear revenue of INR 104520.77 represents *24.92%* of April's total revenue of INR 419417.43. A pie chart can show the share directly because the categories form parts of one total. It should remain simple and use a legend only if multiple categories need to be identified.

### 3. How do the four regions compare on total revenue?

*Chart type: Bar chart.*

This is a categorical comparison of total revenue across North, South, East, and West. A bar chart allows the four regional values to be compared quickly and precisely. The y-axis should start at zero, and 3D effects should be avoided because they can distort visual comparison
